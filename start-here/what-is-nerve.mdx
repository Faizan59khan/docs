Nerve connects your marketing platforms to an AI assistant so you can ask questions about performance, prepare plans and reports, and carry out supported campaign tasks. You can work in the Nerve app or connect Nerve to Claude or ChatGPT.

## What you can do

- **Analyze connected data:** investigate spend, results, pacing, creative performance, and account structure.
- **Plan campaigns:** turn a campaign brief into a prompt with the Media planner, or start with a Prompt library example.
- **Report on performance:** ask for a summary, comparison, or generated report with recommendations and stated assumptions.
- **Monitor a business:** organize supported accounts into advertiser groups for Analytics.
- **Manage supported campaign settings:** request a proposed creation, duplication, budget, or status change, then review its scope before execution.
- **Repeat a process:** save instructions and account sources in a workflow and run it manually or on a schedule.

Availability depends on the platform, account permissions, and the part of Nerve you use. A platform appearing on **Connect accounts** does not mean it supports every task in Analytics, chat, and workflows. Check [integration availability](/connections/supported-integrations).

## Start with one question

Connect one platform, choose an assistant, and begin with a request that only reads data:

```text
For Example Brand on Meta Ads, summarize the last 7 complete days.
Show spend, impressions, clicks, and available conversion metrics.
State the account currency, date range, and missing data.
Do not change anything.
```

Replace the brand with an account you can access. Treat recommendations as a proposal to assess against your business goals. For your first setup, follow [Complete your first Nerve setup](/start-here/first-setup).
