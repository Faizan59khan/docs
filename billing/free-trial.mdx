Review the trial card in **Credits & Billing > Plans** to see the offer and end date for your workspace. A trial is separate from a paid subscription and can have different credits and eligibility.

## Check what your trial includes

Nerve's trial offer is described as **7 days with 200 credits and no card required to start**. Confirm the actual plan, credits, end date, and payment conditions displayed in your workspace before accepting it. Creating another workspace does not establish eligibility for another trial.

1. Open the credits widget in the sidebar, then **Plans**.
2. Review the active trial or available trial offer.
3. Note the remaining credits and end date.
4. Check whether the screen says no charge is scheduled or shows a next charge and saved payment method.

## Continue after the trial

If you want to continue, use the displayed payment-method or subscription action and review its confirmation. Check the plan price, charge date, and whether you are adding a card for later or activating paid billing immediately.

An action such as **Activate plan now** can change when billing begins. Read that confirmation rather than assuming every payment-related button preserves the trial end date.

## If access pauses

Open **Credits & Billing** and inspect the trial or invoice message. Follow the relevant payment action if you want to restore access. After payment, check the plan status and try a small read-only request before resuming scheduled work.

If you do not want to continue, review any available **Cancel trial** action and the payment status shown. Do not assume removing a browser tab or stopping use cancels a saved payment arrangement.

For questions about eligibility, unused credits, or an unexpected charge, contact support with the workspace and trial dates. See [billing and cancellation](/billing/invoices-and-cancellation).
