## Plan comparison

The app displayed the following monthly plans when reviewed on 30 October 2026. Amounts are in US dollars; confirm the total, tax, and terms shown at checkout before paying.

| Plan | Monthly price | Monthly credits | Members | Connected platforms |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Pro** | $19 | 1,900 | 1 | 3 |
| **Agency** | $45 | 4,500 | 3 | 10 |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited |

A platform is a service such as Meta Ads or Google Ads. It is different from an individual advertising account accessible through a connected login. Do not count every ad account as a separate platform when assessing the plan you need.

## Choose based on your workspace

Count the people who need membership and the platform types you need to connect. Review pending invitations if a member-limit message appears. Check the live plan cards for current feature inclusions and any existing subscription terms that differ from this comparison.

Nerve AI replies and workflow runs use workspace credits. External AI subscriptions are separate. Read [credits and usage](/billing/credits) before estimating how much in-app work your team needs.

## Subscribe or change your plan

An Owner or Admin can review the available subscription actions under **Plans**. For a new subscription, follow [payment setup](/billing/payment-methods). If the change you need is unavailable for an existing paid plan, contact support with the current and intended plan rather than creating a duplicate subscription.

For custom capacity or Enterprise requirements, [contact Nerve support](/support-and-privacy/contact-support).
