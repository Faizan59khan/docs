PFBM Analytics appears on Nerve's connection page. Its discoverable analytics tool describes flight-level reporting, spend measures, events, and currency groupings. Confirm what PFBM represents for your organization before using those figures.

## Before you connect

Ask Nerve support to confirm eligibility, the advertiser data included, and the business meaning of each spend measure. Know the flights, date range, currency, and result events required for your report.

## Connect and verify

1. Open **Get started > Connect accounts** and locate **PFBM Analytics**.
2. Review the available action. Confirm any access requirements with support before granting a new connection.
3. Ask your external assistant to discover the PFBM connection and available reporting fields.
4. Confirm the advertiser scope and metric definitions before requesting a report.

## What to ask Nerve

The inspected tool describes flight performance, event filtering, net-to-client and net-to-media spend, and currency grouping. Ask the assistant to explain these fields before calculating efficiency. You do not need to write SQL to ask a business question.

```text
Check available PFBM connections and describe the reporting fields
for Example Brand. Explain net-to-client versus net-to-media spend,
available currencies, event filters, and row limits. Do not query
data until I confirm the advertiser, period, and spend definition.
```

## Check scope and limits

The inspected schema describes a 1,000-row cap and non-additive reach. Do not sum reach across flights as though it were a deduplicated audience total. Ask whether a report was truncated and keep currencies separate. Tool descriptions alone do not verify data access or the meaning of your organization's measures.

If the expected account is missing, use [connection troubleshooting](/support-and-privacy/connection-problems). For expired access, see [Check or reconnect an account](/connections/connection-status).
